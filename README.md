The night runs from doors to last call. Each calendar day has a dare. Earn visor looks. ESC → **STAMP** saves a card of the evening; **RECAP** is what the visor kept. The till is on the pause screen if you want the lights to stay on.

A first-person dusk in Midtown. November 12, 1954. The visor is from later. The sun is still in the street — until it isn't.

Walk a packed floor, take the stairs to a candlelit supper-club lounge, step out onto 47th Street under a wall of towers, or slip out the back into the alley.

Keep walking east past the Corner Set to discover Mercer Pocket Garden, East Avenue, Hawthorne Park and 48th Street. Enter **Blue Note Books**, **Sunrise Bakery** and **Eastern Arcade**. Use **Pockets → The Block** for directions; unfinished deliveries and your collection survive across nights.

The parks have four usable benches. The club plays original jazz-house, while a swung electric-piano quartet follows your walk through the gardens.

## The night

The clock starts at 10:00 PM and runs to after hours. Doors, heat, midnight drop, last call, 2am street. The sky, the crowd, and the house system move with it. Today's Gazette and today's set are not yesterday's.

1. Click **ENTER THE NIGHT** (pointer lock + house system).
2. **WASD** to move, mouse to look, **SPACE** on the LED floor to dance. Stay off the asphalt — moving Checkers will throw you.
3. Walk up to a named person and press **E** to talk. What they say depends on the hour.
4. **Front doors** open onto 47th Street — rain, Checkers, Dottie's, Hotel Astoria. Cross the street for records, tonic, flowers, the Rivoli, gin, a haircut. Subway kiosk for the 12:04. Astoria east stairs for 2F and Frank's ice.
5. **Back doors** dump you in the alley — Vinnie, Muldoon, Socks the cat, fire escape.
6. **Stairs** on the east wall (and the wet fire escape) go up to Velma's lounge.
7. **J / POCKETS** — carried items, deliveries, a portrait, and the block map. Mark a destination for directions.
8. **L / LIFE** — your wallet, paid jobs, apartments, furnishings, and nightclub lighting.
9. **ESC** — deck, visor looks, night stamp, tip the till. Drop MP3 / WAV / FLAC / OGG.

## Shops on 47th

Browse 21 selections across seven businesses. Prices are shown on every card; you start with $120 in fictional game money. Each selection grants its reward once per night, with repeat service and replays included for that night. Use the Uncollected filter to find what is new to you. Aim at merchandise or talk to the shopkeeper and press **E**. On phones, use the on-screen action button.

- **Rex’s Records:** preview three original arrangements, take a sleeve, and deliver it to REXA to change the club set.
- **Lily’s / Midtown Gin:** choose flowers for Velma or a sealed bottle for Marco. Delivered gifts appear in the lounge.
- **47th Pharmacy:** order a fountain drink with a clearly described effect.
- **Tony’s:** preview a haircut, take the chair, and keep the portrait in your notebook and night stamp.
- **The Rivoli:** choose one of three original animated shorts, take a seat, and follow the clue to Dottie’s midnight booth.
- **Dottie’s Diner:** order coffee, pie, or supper and settle in at the counter.

Frank’s ice must be collected from the Astoria 4B machine; Nellie marks the route. Items, errands, your chosen set, and the night’s clock survive reloads. A new calendar day starts a fresh night while keeping earned visor looks. Audio and reduced effects have separate controls.

## A block worth exploring

Every shop now has a **Behind the counter** activity. Follow the daily order slip to assemble a set, fountain drink, bouquet, parcel, portrait, film, or supper tray. Each counter has 27 possible orders. Help at all seven to complete your **passport** and earn BLOCK REGULAR.

Find the **neighborhood board** outside the Astoria end of the block for directions to the new street destinations:

- **The Corner Set**, at the east end near the hotel: listen to three phrases and repeat them with **A / S / D**, or tap the pads. Timing is relaxed, and the notes are shown as letters. Completing the duet earns CORNER GOLD.
- **Mabel’s Night Cart**, at the west end near the diner: chestnuts, pretzels, and cocoa, with one energy benefit per selection per night.
- **47th Camera Club**, at the far east end of the shop sidewalk: borrow a camera, then press **C / CAMERA** while exploring. Your latest six photographs appear in **J → PHOTOS**, where you can remove pictures or save captioned postcards. Photograph four different areas for STREET PHOTOGRAPHER.
- **The midnight payphone**: follow an ordered trail through a silver sleeve at Rex’s, the camera kiosk, and Astoria’s lost-property case. The notebook records the next lead. Finish to discover a new house set and SIGNAL SILVER.

The counter favors, street duet, snacks, camera and mystery are free and optional. Counter stamps, clues, snacks, your best duet, and photographs survive reloads within the same night. A fresh calendar day clears this night’s activity progress while keeping earned visor looks. Photos show only the rendered game; no real camera or screen access is used.

## Work, shop, come home

Press **L / LIFE** for your wallet and goals. This is fictional in-game money; it is separate from the real sponsorship/tip links.

- **Earn:** each first nightly counter favor pays $20. Choose **Paid shifts** inside any shop, clock in, and match three customer order slips for a $45 paycheck. Shifts are repeatable, untimed and saved between visits. After three and six shifts with an employer, you earn $5 raises.
- **Work the club:** the physical **Lighting & soundcheck** console beside REXA’s booth offers soundcheck jobs starting at $55, rising to $65. Visit the workplace to complete orders.
- **Buy a home:** the Astoria property desk and **LIFE → MY HOME** list the Bluebird Studio ($180), Magnolia Flat ($340), and Marigold Suite ($560). Your purchased door opens on the hotel’s second floor. Take the east stairs and follow the room numbers; the notebook can mark your front door. No rent or upkeep.
- **Furnish it:** each shop’s **Home & keepsakes** department carries one permanent piece: a turntable, plant, reading lamp, bar cart, mirror, Rivoli poster, or breakfast table. Purchases appear in your active apartment. You can store/reinstall pieces or move them to another owned home. Buying before you own an apartment stores the item safely.
- **Make the club yours:** choose Electric orchid, Blue velvet, or Golden hour to change the actual floor, washes and laser colors. A permanent $60 game-money patron pass adds Emerald afterparty.

Cash, keys, furniture, job experience, unfinished shifts, and your lighting choice survive reloads and new calendar days. Nightly counter stamps, consumable selections and story activities reset with the night. Your wallet includes a recent transaction ledger and an apartment savings goal.

## Run locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
npm test
```

Needs HTTP because of ES modules. No build step. Three.js r160 is pinned locally in `vendor/three/`, including its MIT license. Fonts use browser fallbacks when unavailable.

For browser regression checks, start the server, then run `npm run test:browser`. This uses `agent-browser@0.37.1` through npx; install its browser with `npx agent-browser@0.37.1 install` if needed. Set `VIBE_TEST_URL` for a different local port. The tests use an isolated browser session and a loopback-only `?inspect=1` API for scene positioning, then exercise the real catalog and dialogue controls. Screenshots are saved to `/tmp/vibe-check-browser`. Run `npm run test:expansion` for the counter, street, mystery, and photography journeys; those screenshots go to `/tmp/vibe-check-expansion`. Run `npm run test:life` for the purchase, pay, apartment, furnishing and nightclub journeys; screenshots go to `/tmp/vibe-check-life`. Run `npm run test:district` for the new streets, benches, umbrella grips, saxophone pose and offline music rendering; screenshots go to `/tmp/vibe-check-district`. Run `npm run test:neighborhood` for the three enterable stores, collections, courier job, arcade game, save recovery and mobile layouts; screenshots go to `/tmp/vibe-neighborhood`.
